Welcome to McNabb's Media Center

Welcome to McNabb Middle School's Media Center new website! We believe Readers are Leaders and we are striving for that goal at McNabb Middle School. Feel free to look around and provde any feedback you may have.

McNabb's Library and Media Center serves as a central hub for literacy, research, and student engagement. Our library provides access to diverse print and digital resources while supporting inquiry-based learning, passion projects, and collaboration across content areas. Our media center plays an essential role in supporting both academic achievement and a culture of reading, serving as a welcoming space where students can explore ideas, develop research skills, and grow as independent learners.
